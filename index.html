On Deleuze

This is a mathematical analogy, not an equation for philosophy. Deleuze’s eternal return is more than a nonclosing orbit: it concerns difference, becoming, affirmation, and selection.

About this study

The geometry runs locally in the browser: Runge–Kutta integration of the Lorenz equations, a freely rotatable 3D projection, and upward Poincaré crossings. This is a mathematical analogy to difference and recurrence, not a literal model of Deleuze's philosophy.
